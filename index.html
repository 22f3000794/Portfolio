About

Research first.
Then scope.

My work starts with the problem, not the feature list — user interviews, competitor benchmarking, community research and review mining, until the pain point is specific enough to build against.

From there I write the requirements that make it buildable: PRDs, user journeys, functional specifications, prioritised backlogs and wireframes, handed to engineering with the decisions and trade-offs documented rather than implied.

Most of my product exposure has been in AI — identifying where a model genuinely improves a workflow, defining what it should explain to the user, and keeping it honest about its own limits. The rest is conventional SaaS, which is a useful corrective: not every problem needs a model.

Nexus AI — enterprise asset intelligence

Enterprise AI, IT asset intelligence & data reconciliation · Team: Garima & Sparsh

Problem & value proposition

Organisations keep employee information, device inventories, identity records and IT assets across disconnected platforms, producing fragmented data, duplicates and mismatched records. Nexus AI builds one reliable view of the IT ecosystem: connect fragmented data, identify inconsistencies, prioritise critical exceptions and keep enterprise records accurate and synchronised.

Reconcile — automated data reconciliation

  • Multi-source integration across configured enterprise systems.
  • People, device, asset and identity reconciliation across HR, identity, device and asset management platforms.
  • Record matching on identifiers and matching attributes.
  • Discrepancy identification — missing records, mismatched attributes, broken relationships.

Connected data sources include Google Cloud Identity, BambooHR, Namely, Miradore, Jira Assets and Jira Service Management.

Exception management — detect, assess, prioritise

  • Detect — apply detection rules, find mismatches, generate evidence-backed exceptions and prevent duplicates across reconciliation runs.
  • Assess — evaluate impact and severity against supporting evidence and source records.
  • Prioritise — calculate risk and prioritisation scores, surface critical exceptions, update existing ones as evidence changes.

Audit & traceability

Track what changed, when, and where it originated; access original source records; maintain an end-to-end history of reconciliation and exception activity, with evidence retained to support audit and compliance requirements.

AI product opportunities

Potential capability extensions, distinct from the confirmed functional workflows above.

  • Intelligent record matching across enterprise systems.
  • Anomaly detection for unusual data patterns.
  • Automated exception classification and risk assessment.
  • AI-generated explanations of technical discrepancies, with suggested remediation.
